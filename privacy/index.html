Digital NCQT - Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital NCQT (the "App"), operated by PSC Media ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses and protects information when you connect your TikTok account to the App.

1. Information we collect

When you log in with TikTok and grant permission, the App may receive only the data covered by the permissions you approve:

  • Basic profile information: display name, avatar, open ID.
  • Profile details: username, bio, profile link, verification status.
  • Account statistics: follower count, following count, likes count, video count.
  • Video information: the list of your public videos and their basic metrics.
  • Videos you choose to upload through the App.

We do not collect your TikTok password.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to:

  • Display your channel information and performance inside the App.
  • Prepare internal content reports for PSC Media.
  • Upload and publish videos to your TikTok account when you instruct us to.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not share it with third parties except service providers that help operate the App under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and data are stored using reasonable technical and organizational security measures and are accessible only to authorized PSC Media staff.

5. Data retention

We keep data only as long as needed to provide the App. When you revoke access, we stop collecting new data and delete stored tokens.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok account settings under security and permissions.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Children

The App is not intended for users under 18 years of age.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, please contact PSC Media via https://pscmedia.vn.
